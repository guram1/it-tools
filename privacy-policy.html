Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how OPS-BOX.net ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit https://ops-box.net.

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use any tool on this site. All tools run entirely in your browser — no input data is sent to our servers.

  • Usage data — pages visited, time on site, browser type, device type, and general geographic location (country/city level) via Google Analytics.

2. Cookies

We use cookies through third-party services:

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by visiting aboutads.info.

This site uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

4. How We Use Your Information

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties except as described above (Google Analytics, Google AdSense). We may share aggregated, non-personally identifiable information publicly.

6. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below or use Google's opt-out tools linked above.
